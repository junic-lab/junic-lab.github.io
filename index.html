Building products for deeply personal choices.

JunicLab is a founder-led product studio in South Korea. We build consumer products that use personal preference and real-world behavior to make everyday decisions more relevant to the individual.

What We Believe

The best answer depends on who is asking.

Popularity and averages are useful signals, but they flatten the differences between people. JunicLab focuses on problems where taking individual preference and observed behavior seriously produces a much better answer.

Current Product

Not just universally popular. Personally perfect.

Our first product is a personalized restaurant discovery service. Instead of treating average ratings as universal truth, it helps people discover places through the preferences and behavior of people with similar tastes. We are starting in Daegu and plan to expand region by region.

Better recommendations start with better evidence.

We separate what a restaurant is like from whether a person prefers it. Recommendations are centered on the relationship between users with similar tastes and the restaurant preference and behavior evidence observed from those users.

Personal over popular

We prioritize what a specific user tends to enjoy over generic popularity and one-size-fits-all rankings.

Evidence beyond averages

We use strong preference signals such as revisit intent instead of treating aggregate ratings as the whole story.

First-party evidence

External data helps establish initial coverage. Long-term value comes from traceable preference evidence generated inside the service as real usage grows.
